Privacy Policy for HOTHEAD

Effective Date: May 17, 2026

HOTHEAD is a helmet heat safety monitoring app developed by Isaiah Cortez. This Privacy Policy explains how HOTHEAD handles information when users use the app.

Information We Collect

HOTHEAD may display helmet sensor readings such as temperature, humidity, heat index, helmet status, connection status, and worker risk score. This information is used to help users monitor helmet heat safety conditions.

Bluetooth and Device Data

HOTHEAD uses Bluetooth to connect to nearby compatible helmet hardware. Sensor data from the helmet is used inside the app to show live readings, status information, and safety alerts.

Data Storage

HOTHEAD may store app-related settings or sensor readings locally on the user's device. Unless future versions add cloud features, this data is not uploaded to our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

HOTHEAD may use Apple, Expo, or related platform services required to build, distribute, test, and operate the app. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

HOTHEAD is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as HOTHEAD changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.
